---
title: Examples
lede: The three worked examples from the specification (§18), each as its source with the ledger the compiler wrote beneath it. They are the integration tests, and every mechanism in the language appears in at least one.
---

Each page shows the program's files, then what `onus review` reported for it: every obligation in the entry module with its state, and the path report for any `path` the program declares. The numbers are read at build time from the review output committed under `examples/` in the repository, so they are the compiler's own, as of the last run that was committed.
